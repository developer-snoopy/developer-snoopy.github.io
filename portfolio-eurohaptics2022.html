EuroHaptics 2022

Conference Publication

Vibrotactile similarity perception in crowdsourced and lab studies

A EuroHaptics 2022 paper comparing haptic similarity studies across lab and crowdsourced environments on iOS and Android devices.

Research Overview

This study examined whether crowdsourcing can serve as a valid method for vibrotactile similarity perception research, and how smartphone platform differences influence the results.

The findings suggested especially strong correlations for iOS-based studies and provided early evidence that remote haptics research can meaningfully complement tightly controlled lab studies.

Key Highlights

  • Oral presentation at EuroHaptics 2022
  • Compared iOS and Android results
  • Focused on similarity perception of rhythmic patterns
  • Built evidence for valid remote haptics studies

Research Significance

The work deepened Dongjae Kwon's research focus on scalable haptic evaluation and helped establish a bridge between experimental rigor and real-world user-device diversity.
