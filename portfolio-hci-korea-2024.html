Special session presentation at HCI Korea 2024

An invited oral presentation introducing research previously published at CHI 2023 to the HCI Korea community.

Talk Overview

This invited session gathered strong SIGCHI papers from 2023 and offered a chance to share the CHI 2023 tacton crowdsourcing work with a domestic HCI audience.

The presentation highlighted the research question, methodological design, and implications for future remote haptics studies.
