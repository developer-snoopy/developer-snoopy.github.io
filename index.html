Profile

Researching interactions through haptics and multimodal interfaces

Dongjae Kwon is currently a Ph.D. student in the Artificial Intelligence Graduate School at Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST). His research focuses on universal and personalized haptic interfaces that enhance user experience across haptic information systems, crowdsourcing, and multimodal user interfaces.

He received his B.S. degree in Optical Engineering from Kumoh National Institute of Technology in 2019 and completed his M.S. degree in Information and Communication Engineering at DGIST in 2021, where he specialized in laser-induced nano-electronics before transitioning into HCI and wearable haptics.
