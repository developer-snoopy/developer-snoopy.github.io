CHI 2023

Conference Publication

Can we crowdsource Tacton similarity perception and metaphor ratings?

A CHI 2023 paper exploring whether crowdsourced evaluation of vibrotactile tactons can produce results comparable to in-lab studies.

Research Overview

High-fidelity vibration actuators in mobile devices make it possible to study tacton perception outside the lab. This work compared lab and crowdsourced evaluations of vibrotactile tactons, focusing on pairwise similarity ratings and metaphor matching.

The study found strong correlations between the perceptual spaces gathered in laboratory and crowdsourced settings, showing promising evidence that online haptic evaluation can be valid when designed carefully.

Key Highlights

  • Published at ACM CHI 2023
  • Oral presentation in Hamburg, Germany
  • Focused on tacton similarity and metaphor ratings
  • Compared lab and crowdsourced haptic evaluation

Why it matters

This project sits at the core of Dongjae Kwon's HCI research direction: scalable, meaningful evaluation of haptic interfaces. It connects methodological rigor with practical opportunities for remote experimentation on real user devices.
