Hi, I'm Dongjae - an HCI researcher exploring touch as a language for interaction

I am a Ph.D. student in the Artificial Intelligence Graduate School at GIST and a member of the Haptic Assistive Media Laboratory.

Grounded in human vibrotactile perception, my work develops reliable and widely deployable haptic interactions for mobile, wearable, and assistive computing platforms.

Research Interests

Human-Computer Interaction / Wearable Haptics / Information Transfer

Grounded in ongoing perceptual research on tactile modalities, my work seeks to establish principled interaction techniques informed by human vibrotactile perception. A central goal is to enable reliable and widely deployable haptic interactions for mobile and wearable computing platforms.
